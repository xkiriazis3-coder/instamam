The place

A meze bar that runs all day

In Lagadikia, just outside Thessaloniki, in a room that was rebuilt from the ground up: marble, wood and low light.

We open early with coffee and carry on with meze, cocktails and drinks until late. No rush, no noise — somewhere you can sit from morning to night without anyone hurrying you along.

The bar is fully stocked, the kitchen works in small plates made for sharing, and the tables are set for company rather than for eating in a hurry.
