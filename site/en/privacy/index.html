2.2 Server logs

Our host automatically records technical data for each request: IP address, timestamp, page requested and browser type. These are used only for security and reliability and are not linked to your identity.

2.3 Cookies and trackers

This website uses no cookies. Nothing is stored on your device — no cookies, no localStorage, no sessionStorage. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel and no advertising tracker of any kind. That is why you will not see a consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Fonts are served from our own server rather than a third-party CDN, so your IP address is not disclosed to anyone else simply by browsing.
